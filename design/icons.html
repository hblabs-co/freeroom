Size ladder and menu bar

Size rules: 1024/512/256 get the full art with 3 glints and motion lines. 128/64 get 2 glints at most and one motion line. At 32 only one larger glint is kept, and 16 has none, with heavier strokes so it stays crisp. The menu-bar template keeps one glint at 18 pt and none at 16 pt.

Why threeOne large glint plus two small ones reads as “sparkling clean” at 1024 and 128 without clutter; the single glint is calmer but flatter.
Agent marker changedIn the app the sparkle no longer marks the AI agent. The agent now uses an amber hex-node glyph , so the brand glints never compete with it.
